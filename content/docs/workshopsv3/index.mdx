# MT Workshops v3
Everything you need as a mechanic, one single script.
Give your mechanics tools to customize, repair, and transform vehicles in an interactive way.

**Features**
- In-Game Creator: Don't know how to code? Don't worry, with our in-game creator you can create any workshop or repair station and manage all vehicle's classes with ease everything without touching a line of code or even leaving the game.
- Every option of the script is optional so you don't need to use what does not fit your server. Choose between classes or normal parts levels, you choose;
- Parts wear: Each part of the car has an wear damage that is given to it based on multiple factors like:
  - Hit damage: Hit damage takes effect on the engine and body of the car, every time you hit a percentage of the part is reduced even more;
  - Mileage: Every few km the parts wear making your players actually maintain your vehicle during its lifetime;
  - Abuse factors: Every part when abused will wear even more, high RPM over a long period of time, agressive braking, offroad driving, etc;
  - Oil & Coolant: Every car has oil and coolant and those need to be replaced when low otherwise it'll damage other parts of the car;
  - Cascading damage: When some part of the car is highly damage, it'll slowly damage the rest of the parts connected to it.
- Repair system: We have multiple ways to repair vehicles:
  - Parts menu (toolbox): You can see all the damage caused in all parts and repair them based on how damage they are, every fix uses necessary materials for it.
  - Repair kit: Mechanics can use this to repair vehicles outside of the workshops or sell to players for them to fix the vehicles wherever they are;
  - NPC Repair stations: You can create repair stations anywhere in the map and decided wether to use them always or only when the mechanics are online.
- Parts menu (toobox): Replace, repair or remove any performance or replaceable part of the car;
- Cosmetics preview: The customer or the mechanic can preview the visual modifications they want to apply in the car before actually applying it;
- Modifications orders: When a preview session is finished, it'll create an order to install the cosmetics parts selected in the car, this also automatically creates an order in the POS;
- POS: Send bills for the customers to pay what they changed, you can either use the default cosmetics added automatically in the preview or create custom orders with only the items you want;
- Classes system: You can easily set a class for a vehicle with our in-game creator menu or with the /addvehicleclass command. This will let your decide different prices for the parts based on the vehicle class and price;
- Crafting: Every item of the workshop can be easily crafted by your mechanics in the workshops using the configured items;
- Nitrous: Install an turbo and nitrous kit and you'll be able to refill and also install the nitrous bottle to use the nitrous in any vehicle (compatible with game controllers);
- Anti lag: Install an anti-lag valve in the vehicle and enjoy the pops and bangs and actual turbo lag delete;
- Cleaning: Clean your vehicle with ease using the cleaning kit. The vehicles cleaned with it will stay cleaned for longer;
- Lights (Xenon & Neon): Install either xenon or neon kits in the car and easily control it with the included lights controller item;
- Extras: Easily toggle extras on any vehicle using the extras controller item, this can be used by any player with the item, for example police;
- Stancing system: Change your vehicle's fitment with ease within our cosmetics preview menu using the stancing kit item;
- Flat bed (tow): Let the mechanics easily tow any car using out interactive flatbed and its clean included UI;
- NPC Missions: Receive calls to repair and modify vehicles from NPCs in different sittuations;
- Management dashboard: Visualize insights and history of every sale made in the workshop, manage employees commissions and products and services prices.
